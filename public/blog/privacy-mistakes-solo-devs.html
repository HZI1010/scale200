5 Privacy Mistakes Every Early-Stage App Makes

July 10, 2026

Privacy compliance is overwhelming, especially when you don’t have a dedicated compliance team. But ignoring it can cost you thousands in fines.

Here are the most common privacy mistakes we see in early-stage apps.

1. No Privacy Policy: If you collect any user data, you need a privacy policy. AI-generated templates are fine to start but customize them.

2. Ignoring GDPR: GDPR applies to anyone with EU users. That’s probably you. Key requirements: consent, data access, right to deletion.

3. Tracking Without Consent: Analytics scripts, cookies, pixels all need user consent in many jurisdictions.

4. Poor Data Storage: Storing user data in plain text, keeping it forever, or not backing it up are all red flags.

5. No Data Deletion Flow: Users have the right to be forgotten. You need a way to delete all their data on request.
